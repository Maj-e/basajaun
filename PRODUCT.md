# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Static HTML/CSS/JS with MapLibre GL JS 4.7.1, no framework, no build; Python pipeline writes static GeoJSON/JSON; GitHub Pages. (Decided by the team, see README.md.)

## Users
- **Primary: the hackathon jury**, watching a short live demo projected in a room, then possibly opening the shared link alone (often on a phone).
- **Must stay credible for professionals**: firefighters and civil-protection staff who would judge whether the tool could serve real intervention.

## Product Purpose
Ulinautas merges NASA FIRMS fire detections from two instruments (MODIS and VIIRS) into one harmonized dataset and shows, on one map, where fires burn, how an episode evolved over time, and who is threatened. Success for the demo: in 10 seconds a viewer understands what, where and when, and remembers the moment the episode exploded.

## Positioning
The harmonization of MODIS (~1 km, confidence 0–100) and VIIRS (~375 m, confidence low/nominal/high) into a single, deduplicated view, replayed as a story of a real episode, built entirely on free NASA and open data.

## Operating Context
- Demo episode: Chile–Argentina border, 33–38° S (central Chile and Mendoza, Argentina), 1–14 Feb 2026, 825 vegetation fires (FIRMS `type 0`; static industrial sources excluded). Script in `docs/demo-script.md`: open on 9 Feb, calm, the spark (11 Feb 20:42 UTC, 1,111 MW, 31× the previous max), the peak (12–13 Feb, 151 and 149 fires/day), close.
- Presenter drives with the keyboard during the pitch (`?demo=1`); the shared link loops on its own for a stand or a lone viewer.
- Projection screen, room lighting and pitch length are unknown.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Available now: real hotspots (position, time, sensor, satellite, confidence, FRP), daily counts, story markers derivable from data.
- Not yet computed (Waves 2–3): grouping into fires, population exposure, alerts, wind. Live mode not wired.
- One front-end developer for the whole UI; the backend precomputes everything the screen shows.
- Terminology is technical and exact (FRP, MODIS, VIIRS, confidence), always shown with a plain gloss, e.g. "FRP (fire intensity)".
- Everything in English.

## Brand Commitments
Name: **Ulinautas**. No logo, palette or typeface exists; the identity is to be proposed, built on the fire-intensity (FRP) palette. Dark theme confirmed by the user.

## Evidence on Hand
Real FIRMS data (`data/raw/`, regenerated by `make data`); figures in `docs/demo-script.md`. No population, wind, alerts or exposure figures exist yet: never show placeholder numbers for them.

## Product Principles
1. The page is the support of a story: the evolution of the episode leads, everything else serves it.
2. Never show a number that was not computed from real data.
3. Exact terms for professionals, plain glosses for everyone else.
4. One place to look at a time during playback.
5. Anything not ready is switched off, not faked.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readable from the back of a room on a projector: large date and counters, strong contrast on the dark theme, identity never by color alone, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, keyboard control.
