What this mockup decides — and what is off

  • Real data. 825 vegetation fires from NASA FIRMS (MODIS + VIIRS), FIRMS type 2 static sources excluded. All figures are computed in the page; the backend will precompute them (daily_summary, hourly_series, story markers).
  • Spark and peak are computed (strongest FRP, day with most fires), never hard-coded: they move with the data.
  • Alerts are off. When population exposure exists (W3-B1), a halo + label appears on the map at the fire, and a counter in this corner keeps the history. No placeholder numbers until then.
  • No "Live" label until live data is wired (Wave 4).
  • Wind will be a hint (arrows), never a forecast (W3-B2).
  • Filters, calendar, layers stay collapsed on the left during playback; they open when paused.
  • Presenter mode: add ?demo=1 to start paused on 9 Feb.
  • Basemap is a built-in land outline (Natural Earth) so the page also works offline.
